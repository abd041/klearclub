Processing Time

Orders are processed within 0-2 business days. Orders placed before 3:00 PM ET (12:00 PM PT) on a business day typically ship the same day; orders after the cutoff, or on weekends and holidays, enter processing the next business day. Every order includes free shipment protection.

  • Cut-off time: 3:00 PM ET (12:00 PM PT)
  • Business days: Monday - Friday only
  • Holidays: Federal bank holidays observed (New Year's, MLK Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas)
