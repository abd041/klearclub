10. Audit, Investigation, and Cooperation

Amino Club may, at any time and at its discretion, review, audit, monitor, archive, or investigate your content, communications, click sources, referred orders, payment behavior, identity, and tax information for compliance with these Terms or applicable law. You agree to cooperate in good faith with any such review, including providing records, taking down content within 24 hours of request, and responding to written inquiries. Failure to cooperate is itself a material breach and grounds for immediate suspension or termination.
